# 微信英语 Bot 管理器图标 v1

图像使用内置 image_gen 生成。用于管理器 EXE、窗口标题栏、任务栏和托盘。
原始 PNG 为透明背景；ICO 只做标准多尺寸转换，保留透明通道，包含 16、24、32、48、64、128、256 像素版本。

设计概念：绿色聊天气泡、字母 A 与书页造型；与现有绿色管理器配色保持一致。

## 实际生成提示词

Use case: logo-brand. Create ONE finished Windows desktop application icon for a Chinese WeChat-connected English tutoring Bot manager. Square 1024x1024 raster asset, with true transparent alpha outside the icon. Design a beautiful Fluent-style emerald green rounded-square app tile, softly rounded bevels and very restrained depth, front-facing and perfectly centered. On the tile place a large warm-white speech bubble whose lower shape subtly resembles an open book; inside is a single bold dark-emerald capital 'A', impeccably legible and centered. A small mint-green offset rounded tab visible behind the main bubble suggests management of multiple bots, but keep the overall mark extremely simple and visually balanced. The A, chat and book concept must remain recognizable at 32px. Premium calm education/productivity aesthetic, forest-to-emerald green, warm ivory and a touch of mint. High contrast, crisp contours, no fine strokes, no busy details. Fill roughly 88% of the canvas, consistent transparent padding. No official WeChat logo, no OpenAI logo, no Chinese text, no app name, no watermark, no mockup, no desktop scene, no grid, no variants, no extra icons, no visible checkerboard background. Only the isolated finished app icon.
